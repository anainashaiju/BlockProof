How BlockProof Works

A simple three-step process for document authenticity.

01

Generate Fingerprint

BlockProof calculates a unique SHA-256 hash from the selected document.

02

Register on Blockchain

The document name and fingerprint are recorded through the smart contract.

03

Verify Authenticity

The document is hashed again and compared with the blockchain record.
